Unincorporated Awareness Initiative • Phase 1 Pilot

Ramsay Hunt Syndrome Awareness

“Life after Ramsay Hunt syndrome: the effects you cannot see.”

1. Our Mission

To raise awareness of Ramsay Hunt syndrome, promote timely recognition and access to care, and help people living with its long-term effects feel understood and less alone.

2. Lived Perspective

Living with the lasting effects of Ramsay Hunt syndrome often involves unseen challenges: relentless tinnitus, severe fatigue from sensory compensation, and hearing loss. Our project provides peer validation and connects patients to trustworthy support networks.

Medical Notice: This project shares lived experience and general awareness information. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support. If experiencing sudden facial weakness or severe ear pain with blistering, seek immediate medical attention via GP, NHS 111, or A&E.

3. Interactive Platform

The interactive web application, featuring the 3D procedural Hope & Transparency Beacon, real-time donation ledger, £5,000 Charity Commission monitor, and donor portal, is compiled and served live in the main application environment.
