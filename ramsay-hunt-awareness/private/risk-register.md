# Risk Register

> **Review Cycle:** Bi-weekly during Phase 1 & 2  
> **Last Evaluated:** 1 November 2026

---

| ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy | Owner | Review Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSK-001 | **Founder Health Fatigue / Flare-up**: Excessive administrative burden worsens long-term symptoms (exhaustion, tinnitus). | Medium | High | Enforce strict 4–6 hr/week ceiling; all communication written; recruit 1 helper early; instant pause protocol without justification. | Founder / AI Agent | 15/11/2026 |
| RSK-002 | **Unintentional Misrepresentation of Charitable Status**: Public or donors infer project is already an official Charity Commission registered charity. | Low | Critical | Clear, unambiguous pre-registration disclosure banner on all web views and receipts ("Unincorporated awareness initiative; not a registered charity"); £5k statutory threshold monitor. | Founder | 15/11/2026 |
| RSK-003 | **Medical Advice Liability / Inaccuracy**: Public or patients mistake lived experience narratives for clinical advice, drug dosing, or guaranteed recovery. | Low | High | Comprehensive clinical disclaimer on every page; separate personal narrative from clinical facts; cite only NHS, Facial Palsy UK, and The Brain Charity primary sources. | Founder | 15/11/2026 |
| RSK-004 | **Financial Transparency & Solvency Breach**: Discrepancies between PayPal receipts and public ledger undermine public trust. | Very Low | High | Automated mathematical reconciliation engine; 100% itemized proof-of-expense receipts; zero administration overhead policy; cryptographic audit checksums. | Founder | 15/11/2026 |
| RSK-005 | **Special Category (Health) Data Breach**: Public contributors submit sensitive medical histories or private health narratives without GDPR consent. | Medium | High | No open-ended medical submission forms at launch; strict privacy policy adhering to UK GDPR Article 6 & Article 9; anonymisation by default. | Founder | 15/11/2026 |
| RSK-006 | **Premature Charity Commission Overhead**: Registering an independent CIO before establishing sufficient operational and trustee capacity. | Medium | Medium | Thorough route evaluation in Phase 1; prioritising an established charity partnership (e.g. Facial Palsy UK) over immediate independent incorporation. | Founder | 15/11/2026 |
