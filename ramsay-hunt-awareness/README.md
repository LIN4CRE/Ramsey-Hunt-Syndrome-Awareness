# Ramsay Hunt Syndrome Awareness Initiative — Project Workspace

> **Status:** Phase 1 Private Foundations (Pre-launch, Unincorporated Awareness Initiative)  
> **Jurisdiction:** England and Wales (Subject to Founder Confirmation)  
> **Prepared:** 1 November 2026  
> **Confidentiality:** Private planning workspace — no materials herein are approved for public distribution or clinical advice without explicit founder sign-off.

---

## Directory Architecture

- **`private/`**: Confidential founder constraints, draft risk registers, initial budgets, partner shortlist, and internal approval logs. Not for public distribution.
- **`planning/`**: One-page project brief, comparison of organisational routes (Campaign vs. Partnered vs. Independent CIO), 4-phase launch roadmap, verified primary source register, and work-package task breakdown.
- **`drafts/`**: Draft copy for founder lived-experience story (with privacy toggles), prospective partner outreach emails, draft awareness social posts, printable A5 leaflet copy, and practical volunteer role description.
- **`policies/`**: Governance documents covering UK GDPR/Article 9 data handling, safeguarding and peer moderation, financial transparency & £5,000 Charity Commission threshold controls, and public corrections/complaints handling.
- **`website/`**: Reference site documentation, content review accessibility checklist, and build artefacts.
- **`approved-for-publication/`**: Air-gapped holding folder. Only items with explicit, documented founder approval are placed here for public dissemination.

---

## Golden Operating Rules

1. **Patient-Led Energy First**: The founder's health and energy limitations (4–6 hours/week) dictate the pace. All updates must be written, structured, and contain no more than 3 decisions at a time.
2. **Strict Accuracy Separation**: Personal lived experience is strictly separated from verified medical facts. No clinical claims, cure promises, or diagnostic guidance.
3. **No Unapproved Public Actions**: No publishing of names, stories, launching live public donation drives, or filing regulatory submissions without prior explicit approval.
