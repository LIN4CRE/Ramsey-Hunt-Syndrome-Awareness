# Project Brief: Ramsay Hunt Syndrome Awareness Initiative

> **Document Type:** One-Page Project Brief (Phase 1 Baseline)  
> **Prepared:** 1 November 2026  
> **Working Title:** Life after Ramsay Hunt syndrome: the effects you cannot see  
> **Author:** Patient-Led Initiative in Collaboration with AI Support

---

## 1. Mission Statement
“To raise awareness of Ramsay Hunt syndrome, promote timely recognition and access to care, and help people living with its long-term effects feel understood and less alone.”

---

## 2. Core Problem & Unmet Need
- **Delayed Diagnosis & Public Invisibility:** Ramsay Hunt syndrome (herpes zoster oticus) frequently suffers from misdiagnosis or delayed antiviral/corticosteroid intervention. While facial weakness may gradually improve, the long-term, invisible sequelae—persistent debilitating exhaustion, intractable tinnitus, hearing impairment, vestibular dysfunction, and neuropathic pain—are widely misunderstood by employers, friends, and the broader public.
- **Patient Isolation:** Individuals living years post-infection often feel isolated in managing complex energy budgeting and sensory loss without accessible, tailored community signposting.

---

## 3. Initial Target Audiences
1. **Affected Individuals & Families:** Providing relatable lived-experience narratives, peer validation, and practical coping signposts.
2. **Friends, Employers & General Public:** Fostering empathy and understanding regarding invisible fatigue, auditory processing challenges, and fluctuating capacity.
3. **Healthcare Practitioners & Audiology Units:** Offering balanced patient-perspective awareness materials that complement established clinical pathways.

---

## 4. Phase 1 & 2 Pilot Scope (What We Are Doing)
- Publish one consented, highly accessible founder lived-experience story alongside clear, verified clinical links.
- Maintain a digital-first, zero-overhead awareness portal with real-time financial transparency and 100% receipt reconciliation.
- Establish contact with at least one established UK charity (e.g. Facial Palsy UK or The Brain Charity) to explore formal collaboration or hosting.
- Recruit one practical volunteer helper to ensure the founder's energy cap (4–6 hrs/week) is protected.

---

## 5. Explicit Non-Goals (What We Are NOT Doing)
- **No Clinical or Diagnostic Advice:** We never offer diagnostic opinions, medical prescriptions, or clinical prognosis.
- **No Crisis or 24/7 Helpline Services:** We do not provide round-the-clock crisis counseling or medical triage.
- **No Premature Claims of Charitable Registration:** We operate transparently as an unincorporated awareness initiative until formal registration criteria and trustee capacity are met.
- **No Commercial Merchandising or Opaque Admin Fees:** 100% of public support is directly allocated to verified educational and printing costs.
