# Phased Launch Plan (Pilot to Sustainability)

> **Philosophy:** Health-paced, phased progression. Work in manageable cycles. Silence is never interpreted as approval.

---

## Phase 1: Private Foundations (Weeks 1–2) — CURRENT
- **Focus:** Preparation, verified sourcing, boundary setting, and private drafting.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - One-page project brief (`planning/project-brief.md`).
  - Verified primary source register (`planning/source-register.csv`).
  - Partner shortlist and draft initial outreach emails (`drafts/partner-emails.md`).
  - Strategic route comparison document (`planning/route-options.md`).
  - Founder story draft with privacy attribution options (`drafts/founder-story.md`).
  - Initial 4-point financial transparency ledger and threshold monitor.
- **Approval Gate 1:** Founder approves mission text, privacy boundary choices, and authorizes which partner outreach emails may be sent.

---

## Phase 2: Pilot Preparation (Weeks 3–4)
- **Focus:** Assembling low-maintenance, accessible public materials in a private environment.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Accessible, WCAG-compliant awareness web hub and transparency portal.
  - Printable A5 awareness leaflet text (`drafts/awareness-leaflet.md`).
  - Six educational awareness social media posts (`drafts/social-posts.md`).
  - Written role description for practical volunteer helper (`drafts/volunteer-role.md`).
  - Core governance policies (Privacy/GDPR, Safeguarding, Financial Controls, Corrections).
- **Approval Gate 2:** Founder approves exact public text, contact channel, helper recruitment post, and confirms initial digital publication targets.

---

## Phase 3: Controlled Pilot (Weeks 5–8)
- **Focus:** Soft launch, partner listening, and workload monitoring.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Publish approved founder story and verified clinical resource links.
  - Dispatch approved partner introductory emails to shortlisted organisations.
  - Onboard 1 practical helper to manage document formatting and task tracking.
  - Bi-weekly energy check: ensure founder hours remain within 4–6 hrs/week.
  - Monitor all financial transactions against the £5,000 Charity Commission threshold.
- **Approval Gate 3:** Review partner feedback and founder capacity; decide whether to remain a permanent campaign, partner formally, or plan independent charity registration.

---

## Phase 4: Long-Term Scaling or Formal Incorporation (Weeks 9–12+)
- **Focus:** Formal partnership agreement OR independent CIO preparation.
- **Key Deliverables (If CIO selected):**
  - Recruitment pack for 3 independent trustees (finance, lived experience, governance).
  - Charity Commission model constitution drafting.
  - HMRC Gift Aid registration preparation.
