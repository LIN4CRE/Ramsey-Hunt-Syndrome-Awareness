# Strategic Route Comparison: Campaign vs. Partnered vs. Independent Charity

> **Purpose:** Evaluation framework for the founder to decide the optimal legal and operational vehicle.  
> **Jurisdiction:** England and Wales (Charity Commission / HMRC rules)  
> **Date:** 1 November 2026

---

## Executive Summary & Recommendation

| Feature | Route A: Small Awareness Campaign (Current) | Route B: Project Hosted by Existing Charity (Recommended) | Route C: Independent Registered Charity (CIO) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Governance Burden** | Minimal. Self-governed with founder and 1 helper. | Low for founder. Host charity provides established legal, trustee, and accounting umbrella. | High. Requires minimum 3 independent trustees, regular board meetings, statutory filings. |
| **Founder Energy Demand** | Very Low (2–4 hours/week). Focused purely on content and lived experience. | Low–Medium (3–5 hours/week). Collaborative meetings and review. | High (8–15+ hours/week). Founder carries heavy administrative, governance, and compliance burden. |
| **Financial & Tax Requirements** | Unincorporated association rules apply if charitable. Must register with Charity Commission if gross annual income reaches **£5,000**. No Gift Aid unless recognized by HMRC. | Donations received by host charity directly; can claim Gift Aid immediately; transparent project fund ring-fencing. | CIO must register with Charity Commission regardless of income (£0 threshold). Can register with HMRC for Gift Aid. Annual returns mandatory. |
| **Legal Liability** | Unincorporated association members can face personal liability if contracts or debts are incurred. | Host charity carries legal liability and holds required insurances. | CIO provides limited liability corporate structure for trustees. |
| **Speed to Launch** | Immediate (Days). | Moderate (4–8 weeks to agree written memorandum of understanding). | Slow (3–6+ months for trustee recruitment, constitution drafting, and Charity Commission review). |
| **Long-Term Sustainability** | Vulnerable if founder experiences health flare-ups or fatigue. | Highly resilient. Supported by existing charity infrastructure and continuity. | Resilient if strong board recruited; vulnerable if dependent on solo founder. |

---

## Detailed Analysis of Routes

### Route A: Small Patient-Led Awareness Campaign (Unincorporated Pilot)
- **When it fits:** Testing initial public interest, publishing personal narrative, building initial digital resources without premature overhead.
- **Key Caveats:** Must maintain meticulous transparency if donations are accepted. Must track income closely against the **£5,000 Charity Commission registration threshold**.
- **Assessment:** Excellent starting point for Phase 1 & 2.

### Route B: Project Hosted by an Existing Charity (e.g. Facial Palsy UK or The Brain Charity) — **RECOMMENDED**
- **When it fits:** When the initiative's objectives align closely with established national charities that already possess medical advisory boards, data protection infrastructure, and Gift Aid registration.
- **Key Steps to Agree:** Written agreement covering naming, intellectual property, donation ring-fencing, founder energy constraints, and exit terms.
- **Assessment:** Protects the founder's health while providing immediate credibility, clinical review safety, and sustainable reach.

### Route C: Independent Registered Charity (Charitable Incorporated Organisation - CIO)
- **When it fits:** When a distinct, long-term gap exists that established charities cannot accommodate, accompanied by a committed board of 3+ experienced trustees.
- **Key Steps to Agree:** Drafting CIO constitution (Foundation or Association model), formal trustee recruitment, HMRC recognition, and statutory Charity Commission application.
- **Assessment:** Keep as a potential Phase 4 milestone only if partner exploration confirms a distinct independent charity is essential.

---

## Proposed Decision Gate
1. **Pilot Phase 1 & 2** as an unincorporated awareness campaign (Route A).
2. Concurrently initiate exploratory written dialogue with **Facial Palsy UK** and **The Brain Charity** regarding Route B.
3. Review partner responses before committing any resources to Route C.
