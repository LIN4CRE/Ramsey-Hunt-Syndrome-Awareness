# Moderation and Safeguarding Policy (Draft)

> **Purpose:** Safeguarding standards and moderation guidance for public communications and patient interactions.

---

## 1. Safety and Clinical Boundaries
- **No Crisis Response:** The initiative does not provide an emergency crisis or clinical response service. If anyone indicates acute mental health distress or medical emergency, standard referral text must be provided immediately:
  - *Emergency Medical:* Call 999 or attend your nearest NHS A&E.
  - *Non-Emergency Health:* Call NHS 111.
  - *Mental Health Support:* Samaritans (Call 116 123) or Shout (Text SHOUT to 85258).
- **No Peer Medical Prescribing:** In comments, emails, or discussions, members must not recommend prescription medications (e.g. specific antiviral doses, off-label steroids) or promise clinical cures.

---

## 2. Public Channel Moderation Standards
Public communications on all digital platforms must adhere to:
1. **Respect and Dignity:** Zero tolerance for abusive, discriminatory, or harassing behaviour.
2. **Privacy Protection:** Immediate redaction or deletion of comments containing third-party personal medical details, contact numbers, or addresses posted without consent.
3. **Misinformation Filtering:** Any claims asserting unproven cures, anti-vaccine conspiracy claims regarding varicella, or dangerous therapies will be removed immediately.

---

## 3. Vulnerable Adults and Escalation
Where concern arises regarding the safety of an individual:
- The situation must be documented in the internal register.
- Appropriate statutory safeguarding signposting will be supplied in writing.
- Any unresolved risk will be escalated to the founder or appointed trustee immediately.
