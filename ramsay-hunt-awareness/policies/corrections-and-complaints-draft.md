# Public Corrections and Complaints Procedure (Draft)

> **Purpose:** Transparent, rapid mechanism for addressing feedback, factual errors, or concerns regarding published materials.

---

## 1. Commitment to Accuracy
We are committed to the highest standards of medical accuracy, clarity, and respect. If any published statement is found to be out of date, inaccurate, or misleading, we will correct it swiftly.

---

## 2. Procedure for Raising a Concern or Correction
- **Contact:** Send a written notification to the project contact email with the subject line *"Correction Request"* or *"Feedback"*.
- **Details Required:**
  - The URL or document title.
  - The specific sentence or statement in question.
  - Supporting primary reference or clinical guideline citation where applicable.

---

## 3. Resolution Timeline
1. **Acknowledgement:** Within 2 business days of receipt.
2. **Review:** The statement will be checked against verified primary sources (NHS, Facial Palsy UK, The Brain Charity) and discussed with the founder or clinical reviewer.
3. **Action:** If an error is identified, an update will be published within 48 hours of review, accompanied by an explicit changelog note explaining what was corrected.
