# Financial Controls and Transparency Policy (Draft)

> **Governing Framework:** Charity Commission Guidance (CC8 - Internal Financial Controls) & Fundraising Regulator Standards

---

## 1. Statutory Threshold Monitoring (£5,000 Rule)
- Under the Charities Act 2011 (England and Wales), an unincorporated organisation established for charitable purposes must register with the Charity Commission once its gross annual income reaches **£5,000**.
- **Automated Monitoring:** Our platform incorporates a real-time statutory threshold monitor. At 80% (£4,000), an automated warning triggers a formal review of legal structures (e.g. accelerating Route B partnership or preparing Route C CIO incorporation).

---

## 2. Inflow Processing & PayPal Integration
- **Direct Processing:** Inflows received via `PayPal.me/dlinacre16` are logged against the public transparency ledger.
- **Dedicated Allocation:** Every contribution is designated for verified project campaigns (e.g. educational leaflet printing, accessible web infrastructure, patient community outreach).
- **No Gift Aid Claims Pre-Registration:** Because the initiative is in a pre-registration pilot phase, Gift Aid declarations cannot legally be claimed. Donors are clearly informed of this before contributing.

---

## 3. Expense Controls & 100% Receipt Reconciliation
- **Pre-Approval:** No project expenditure may occur without prior founder approval.
- **Zero Administrative Skimming:** 0% of public donations are used for administrative profit, executive perks, or opaque overhead.
- **Itemized Proof-of-Expense:** Every expenditure must be accompanied by an itemized merchant receipt, timestamp, and audit classification before being recorded in the public ledger.
- **Cryptographic Audit Checksums:** The ledger uses deterministic hashing (`AUDIT-HASH`) to prevent tampering or back-dated adjustments.

---

## 4. Financial Records Retention & Reporting
- All transaction records and digital receipt scans are retained for a minimum of 6 years in accordance with HMRC guidelines.
- Publicly downloadable CSV ledgers and audit summaries are generated on-demand for donors and regulatory bodies.
