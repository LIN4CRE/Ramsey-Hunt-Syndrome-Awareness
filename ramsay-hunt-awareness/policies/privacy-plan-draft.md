# Privacy and Data Protection Plan (Draft)

> **Governing Standards:** UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018  
> **Special Category Considerations:** Article 9 (Health Data)

---

## 1. Operating Principle: Data Minimization
The initiative operates on a strict data-minimization approach:
- We do not run open public health surveys, unmoderated community bulletin boards, or automated mailing lists during Phase 1 & 2.
- No health data is collected unless explicitly submitted for a consented lived-experience story.

---

## 2. Handling Contributor Stories & Health Information
- **Lawful Basis:** Article 6(1)(a) — Explicit Consent; Article 9(2)(a) — Explicit Consent for Special Category Health Data.
- **Granular Consent Options:** Contributors must be given separate, granular choices for:
  - Text story publication.
  - Photographic or audio/video publication.
  - Anonymity, first name attribution, or full attribution.
- **Right to Withdraw:** Contributors may withdraw consent at any time. Upon receipt of a withdrawal request, materials on digital channels under our control will be removed within 5 working days. The limitations of recalling third-party prints or external shared copies will be transparently explained in advance.
- **No Children's Health Data:** We do not collect or publish stories from individuals under 18 during the pilot phase.

---

## 3. Financial Donor Privacy
- We record only transaction identifiers, date, category, and public contributor display preferences.
- Contributor names may be displayed as "Anonymous Contributor" or chosen first name.
- Financial transactions are processed via PayPal's secure infrastructure; our platform never stores banking credentials, credit card numbers, or PayPal account passwords.

---

## 4. Subject Access and Enquiries
All privacy requests, access inquiries, or rectification demands should be directed in writing to the project contact email. Responses will be provided within statutory timeframes (1 calendar month).
