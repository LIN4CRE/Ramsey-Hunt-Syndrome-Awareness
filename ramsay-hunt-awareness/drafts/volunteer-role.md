# Volunteer Role Description: Awareness Project Helper

> **Role Title:** Awareness Project Practical Helper  
> **Status:** Voluntary / Unpaid Helper  
> **Time Commitment:** 2–3 hours per week (Flexible, remote)  
> **Purpose:** Share administrative, research, and formatting tasks so the founder can contribute effectively without health fatigue or burnout.

---

## Key Responsibilities
- **Document Formatting & Maintenance:** Organize drafts, format printable flyers, and keep project trackers updated in Markdown or CSV formats.
- **Secondary Research:** Assist in verifying publicly available contact details for prospective charity partners, audiology clinics, and patient forums.
- **Draft Scheduling:** Prepare social media post drafts and portal updates using founder-approved text templates.
- **Accessibility Checks:** Test digital materials with screen readers and keyboard navigation to ensure full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.

---

## Role Boundaries & Restrictions
- **No Unsupervised Clinical Statements:** Must never provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations to contributors or the public.
- **No Direct Financial Authority:** Helpers do not handle bank accounts, solicit unauthorized funds, or sign financial commitments.
- **Strict Privacy & Data Handling:** Unsupervised collection of personal or sensitive health data is prohibited. All enquiries must be handled according to our Privacy Plan.
- **Approval Gate:** No material may be published, emailed, or sent to external parties without explicit founder approval.

---

## What We Offer
- Full flexibility around personal health, caring responsibilities, or work.
- Practical experience in patient advocacy, accessible communications, and open financial transparency.
- A supportive, respectful environment with zero unrealistic deadlines.
