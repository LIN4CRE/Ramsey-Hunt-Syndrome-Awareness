## Story Text (Draft Option A: Anonymous / First Name)

For more than eight years, I have lived with the lasting effects of Ramsay Hunt syndrome.

My experience began with shingles in my right ear and a delayed diagnosis. Today, exhaustion, tinnitus and hearing loss affect everyday life, and I use a hearing aid.

What can be hardest is feeling that other people cannot see or understand the effort ordinary activities require. In public, you might look fine, but underneath you are managing sensory distortion, ringing ears, and a level of neurological fatigue that doesn't simply resolve with a good night's sleep.

I want to help change that—not only by telling my story, but by helping others find reliable information, understand their symptoms, and feel less alone.

This project is starting small, focusing on awareness, validation, and connecting people to established support networks. My hope is that greater understanding will lead to earlier recognition and more compassionate support for people living with the long-term, invisible effects.
