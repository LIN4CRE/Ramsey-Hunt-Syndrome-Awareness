## 3. Outreach to Local GP Practice / Audiology Department

**Subject:** Patient-led awareness materials: life after Ramsay Hunt syndrome  

Hello,

I am developing a small patient-led awareness initiative based on my eight-year experience of living with the long-term effects of Ramsay Hunt syndrome, including hearing loss, tinnitus, and fatigue.

Would your clinical team consider reviewing a concise patient-experience awareness leaflet for display in your waiting or audiology consultation areas, or sharing established specialist resources (such as Facial Palsy UK)?
